# Tool tests and authorizations (member handbook)

Some tools need a test before you can use them on your own. This page explains how that works. Your trainer always has the last word.

## Where to find it

Sign in, then open **Members > Tool authorizations** (`/members/training`). Each tool shows where you stand:

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Take the test | You can start it | Press **Take the test** |
| Resume | You have a test open | Press **Resume**; the time left is shown |
| Cooldown | You failed recently | Wait; the page says how long |
| Locked | You've used all your tries | Talk to a trainer |
| Awaiting trainer | You passed | A trainer will contact you to finish your sign-off |
| Authorized | You can use the tool | Nothing more to do (until a retake date, if one is shown) |
| Retake by [date] | The test changed and you need to pass the new one | Press **Retake the test** before the date |
| Lapsed | The retake date passed without a pass | Pass the new test to get your authorization back |

## Taking a test

- Your answers are saved as you go. If your connection drops or you close the tab, come back to Training and press **Resume**.
- The clock is on the server. The "Time left" shown on the page is a guide; the real limit is the one the server keeps.
- If you were given **extra time** by a trainer, the page says so. The time limit is multiplied for your test only.
- If you were given **read-aloud** tools, the page says so. Your screen reader or read-aloud tool is allowed for that test.
- If time runs out, the answers saved so far are marked. Save often.
- Some questions show a picture. Every picture has a text description, and every drag-and-drop question also works with the keyboard and by tapping.

## After you submit

- You see your score. Depending on the test, you may also see which questions you missed. Correct answers are shown only where the test allows it, so a failed try can't be used to learn the answers.
- Passing does **not** give you the authorization. It puts you in a trainer's queue. A trainer does a hands-on check and signs you off.
- If a trainer sends you back, they tell you what to work on. You can retake the test or keep training.
- Some tests have a limited number of tries, and a wait between tries. The test page and the tool's card on the Training page show which try you're on (for example "Attempt 2 of 3 (retake 1)"). After the last try, talk to a trainer, who can ask a lead to reset your count.

## Your record

On the Training page, **Download my training record** gives you a JSON file with your tests, attempts, certifications, authorizations, retake dates and any notes your trainer has marked "member can read". It does not include a trainer's private notes or staff email addresses. Your download is logged in your own access history.

Staff who look at your training record (for example, to answer a request or to investigate a dispute) must give a reason, and that access is logged and reported to the board, the same as other personal information.

## Announcements (optional)

If you want a congratulations post in the club Discord when you're signed off, switch it on in the Training page. It's off by default. Choose whether to show your first name only, or your first name and Discord username. Turning it off stops future posts. Posts already made stay on Discord; ask an admin to remove one if you want it gone.

## Questions

Ask a trainer for the tool, or ask at the front desk. For a change to your record, contact a trainer or the training lead.
