# Trainer guide

You review members who passed a test on a tool you're assigned to, do the hands-on check, and sign them off. Your sign-off is the only thing that gives a member the authorization. The test result alone never does.

## Your queue

**Admin > People > Training** lists members waiting for you. You see only the tools you're assigned to (ask a lead to add you under **Trainers**). Tabs:

- **Ready for review**: passed, not yet started.
- **In training**: you or another trainer have started hands-on training.
- **Recently certified**: signed off in the last 90 days.

The "waiting days" column shows how long someone has been waiting. Nothing escalates automatically yet, so chase long waits yourself.

## A member's file

Open a row to see the file:

1. **Their test**: each question with what they answered, whether it was right, and the key. You see the key; members never do. Critical questions are marked.
2. **Notes**: add a dated note. Tick **Member can read it** to share it with them. Tick **This was a training session** for hands-on time. Notes can't be edited. If you got something wrong, add a new note that corrects it.
3. **Actions**:
   - **Start training**: moves them from passed to in-training and tells them.
   - **Mark ready**: they're ready for sign-off.
   - **Sign off…**: gives the authorization. Say what you did with them. If the tool needs more than one sign-off, the page shows how many are still needed. You can't sign off your own training.
   - **Needs more…**: sends them back with a reason they can read. They can retake the test or keep training.
4. **Accommodations**: set extra time (×1.25, ×1.5 or ×2) and whether read-aloud tools are allowed, with a note. This applies to every test they start after you save it. It doesn't change any test they've already started, and it doesn't change grading. You can't set your own.
5. **Evidence for this attempt**: a JSON file of the attempt exactly as stored: their answers, the scoring, the test version and its fingerprint, and the time and network details. Use it when a result is disputed. Asking for it needs a reason and is logged. It holds only the last saved answers, not earlier autosaves.

## Record what you did

For each member, the note history should be enough for someone else to see:

- what you covered in the hands-on session,
- where they need work,
- when you signed them off and what you checked.

## What you can't do

- Sign off your own training, or override it.
- Change a test. Tests belong to authors; tell the training lead if a question is wrong.
- Give yourself an accommodation.


## Starting someone without a test

If your space doesn't use online tests (or a tool has none), open **Training** and press **+ Start hands-on training with someone**. Find the member, choose the tool, and press Start. They are told, and from there it's the normal path: add notes, mark ready, sign off. You can only do this for tools you train, never for yourself.
