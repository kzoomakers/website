Woodshop

Jointer Safety and Operation

NOTICE: Rules and guidelines listed on this page are only reminders. Persons must read safety manual, machine manual, and be trained by PPM Wood-shop Captain.
Access: Safety orientation required PPE: Eye protection, hearing protection

Safety Rules

  • Safety glasses and hearing protection are required.
  • Before turning on the machine, make adjustments for depth of cut and position of the fence.
  • Do not adjust the out-feed table or remove the guard without Wood-shop Captain's approval.
  • The maximum cut for jointing an edge is 1/8" and for a flat surface (face) it is 1/16".
  • Stock must be at least 12" long and at least 3/8" thick unless a special feather board is used.
  • Feed the work piece so the knives will cut "with the grain."
  • DO NOT PLANE END GRAIN!
  • Inspect the wood before sending through the jointer. Use only new stock that is free of knots, splits, cracks and fasteners.
  • Keep your hands away from the cutter-head even though the guard is in position. Maintain at least a 6" margin of safety!
  • Use a push stick or push block when planing a flat surface (face).
  • Do not apply pressure directly over the knives with your hand.
  • The jointer knives must be sharp. Dull knives will vibrate the stock and may cause kickbacks.
